# NMM Holdings Design System

## Reference and intent

Adapt the editorial confidence, high-contrast campaign photography and product-first framing from the supplied Concept 02 storefront still. Keep NMM's gold identity, Melton's portraits, Polar catalog, services, work examples and contact details intact.

## Visual rules

- Palette: ink `#080807`, charcoal `#10100e` and `#151512`, satin gold `#d9b86b`, pale gold `#f0d89a`, warm ivory `#eeeae0`, muted stone `#aaa79c`.
- Typography: Playfair Display for editorial headlines, DM Sans for reading text, Manrope for labels and controls. Create hierarchy with scale and whitespace; keep body copy unembellished.
- Layout: full-bleed founder portrait, centered hero identity, generous section padding, horizontal Polar rail, service cards, paired project previews and portrait-led contact. Preserve distinct shopping and service enquiry paths.
- Material: layered near-black surfaces, fine gold rules and restrained warm light. The gold logo stands alone with no tile, centered in the mobile header.
- Imagery: retain the founder portrait in monochrome and Polar product images in color. Photographs carry the visual story.

## Motion rules

- Signatures: extruded Three.js gold NMM mark with pointer tilt and scroll rotation; slow founder-photo parallax and a clipped image reveal.
- Supporting motion: staggered viewport reveals, restrained product image tilt, quick button feedback and a responsive cart drawer. Animate transforms and opacity; use smooth, interruptible interaction timing.
- Keep controls usable immediately. Start the 3D renderer only when visible, cap pixel density on phones, pause off-screen and when the tab is hidden. Keep the original transparent logo as a fallback. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior

Use semantic landmarks, useful image descriptions, visible focus and readable contrast. Keep nav and cart keyboard operable. Center the standalone brand mark on small screens with the menu at the right; retain touch scrolling for the product rail.
